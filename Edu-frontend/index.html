N° 03 — Two ways to teach

In person — offline mode

The teacher speaks once. Every child hears their own language.

A real teacher talks at the front of the room. Our site listens on each child's device and returns the lesson in their mother tongue, live.

Online — live classroom

Or the room opens up, and everyone picks their language.

The teacher starts a class from our site and shares a join code. Every student joins the same meet and picks the language they listen in.

How each mode works

Two modes, one classroom

Offline mode

The teacher is in the room

The child opens the lesson on a phone or a shared tablet and picks their language once. From then on the teacher just teaches — no app to drive, no button to press mid-sentence.

  • Speech in. The device hears the teacher as they talk.
  • Translated out. The sentence comes back spoken in the child's own language.
  • Around 2.4 seconds from spoken word to spoken translation.
  • One language per session — set it once at the start.
Online mode

The classroom is a link

The teacher creates a class on our site and gets a six-character join code. Students enter the code, pick a listening language, and join the same live video room.

  • Everyone chooses. Twenty students, twenty independent language choices.
  • Translated once per language, not once per student — so twenty Hindi listeners cost the same as one.
  • Video and voice stay live while the translated audio arrives alongside it.
  • Latency stays flat as more languages join the room.
